- Base: `cc73e63ac9cb3ac32a404b09bf95e84acb92866b` on `cuongnk/implement-api-contract`.
- Join persists `ActivityRecruitmentApplication` as PENDING, without an assignment. One row per position/user, explicit versioned resubmission after rejection/removal, append-only execution audit.
- `POST /api/activities/{id}/recruitment/applications/{applicationId}/review` accepts expectedVersion, applicationVersion, decision APPROVED/REJECTED, reason. Rejection requires a reason. Current club management or an active, currently eligible HOST assignment is required, including receipt replay. Self-review is forbidden. Verification authority alone does not grant recruitment review.
- Approval rechecks publication, activity/semester availability, open recruitment, position, current account/campus/membership, duplicate assignment, position capacity and approved action capacity. Serializable transaction, execution/application concurrency tokens, unique indexes and idempotent receipts protect the last seat and repeated delivery. Only approval creates an assignment.
